# Changes relative to the submitted version

The camera-ready paper stands on its own and does not describe its history. This file does. The reviews, the rebuttals and the decision are on [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=2BB0oOmLs4). The run rules with their dated amendments are in `docs/camera_ready/RUN_RULES.md`. Appendix letters below are those of the camera-ready paper.

The camera-ready version reruns, at one code version, every GuacaMol method that the current code can still run, reruns LassoBench in one environment, and reports every primary comparison with the task as the unit (NAS-Bench-101, a single task, is tested over seeds). The rows that could not be rerun (marked † in Table 1), the codec comparison and the runs with N = 1000 are stored runs of the submitted version. Every table, or every block of a table, uses one matched set. Of the numbers of the submitted version that map to a number of the camera-ready version, 170 changed at the printed precision and 124 did not. No comparison that was significant in the submitted Tables 1 and 2 changes sign.

## Main changes

- **Title.** "Match the Geometry, Skip the Surrogate" became "Match the Geometry Before the Surrogate". The rebuttal announced a larger revision of the title. The new title stays close to the submitted one, as the handbook asks, and the abstract and the conclusion state the scope.
- **Central claim.** The submitted version claimed that the scaffold, not the surrogate, carries the gain. This claim is replaced by the interaction between support and acquisition maximizer of Section 4.3. The submitted box-versus-shell rows changed the acquisition maximizer together with the support.
- **Statistical unit.** Primary tests now use the 11 tasks, not the 440 cells, because the cells of one seed share their cold-start molecules. The GuacaMol cell-level p-values below 10^-18 of the submitted text are no longer printed.
- **Submitted claims that do not hold.** Not all constant-angle schedules of GeoWalk are worse than the default (Appendix B). GeoCMA does not lead on LassoBench at every dimension (Appendix G). The submitted version bounded the per-task differences between GeoWalk and the GP on the shell by 0.012, and they now reach 0.0135. Their mean is now -0.0001 and is not significant in either version. The cross-codec ordering of the submitted Appendix E is withdrawn (Appendix E).
- **Baselines.** The submitted Papenmeier and Xu box rows were the same runs, and the MSR lengthscale was initialized at sqrt(D) instead of 0.1 sqrt(D); both are corrected. The rows marked § in Table 1 are now the published recipes, which follow the authors' released code; the adapted recipes behind the submitted § rows appear, corrected, as the box arms of Table 2. The other rows adapt the cited methods. Linear BO and the GP on the shell are added to Table 1. The Linear BO port on the shell, posted in the rebuttal, is in the full table, in Table 9 and in Figure 1, not in Table 1 (authors' decision of 2026-11-04, RUN_RULES row 2026-11-04 15:30), and the caption of Table 1 no longer states the cell count of the BAxUS row, which Section 3.1 gives. The box is now the per-coordinate range from the 1st to the 99th percentile of 50,000 encoded ZINC-250k molecules; the submitted text described a minimum-maximum range over the full set.
- **LOL-BO, InvBO, TuRBO, BAxUS and CMA-ES in Table 1.** The submitted rows were stored runs of a wrapper that no longer exists in the repository (recovered from the history and audited on 2026-11-02). In the LOL-BO and InvBO runs, out-of-vocabulary SELFIES stereo tokens raised an error that the wrapper swallowed, so the end-to-end VAE update of LOL-BO and the latent inversion of InvBO never ran; both rows measured something other than the published methods and are not printed. The paper keeps the VAE fixed, so methods that update it are not compared. The BAxUS wrapper projected the cold starts into the target space without normalisation, so its candidates collapsed to one corner of the box in the first phase; the camera-ready row is a rerun of the BoTorch tutorial implementation (arm `baxus_cr`, 439 of 440 cells, because the GP fit failed in one cell). CMA-ES ran with the pycma default population of 20 for 25 proposals, so the covariance was updated once; the camera-ready row uses population 5 (arm `cmaes_full_rd_pop5`), and the population-20 run (`cmaes_full_rd`) is in the full table. TuRBO is the published-recipe run (`turbo_published`). The stored rows and their audit are in the results bundle; the task-level GeoWalk lead over each of these rows is in `docs/baselines/tab_main_full.tex`.
- **COWBOYS and SAASBO.** The COWBOYS row of the submitted Table 1 is dropped, because the stored run did not implement the published method. The published method draws latent points from the VAE prior and refines them by Markov chain Monte Carlo. The stored run instead drew Gaussian candidates around the centroid of the cold-start latents, far outside the shell. The SAASBO row, which covers 10 seeds, is printed only in the full table (`docs/baselines/tab_main_full.tex`).
- **The posted "smaller margin".** The rebuttal stated that the shell version of the corrected MSR recipe still outperforms its box version, by a smaller margin. The shell is now ahead by +0.0331, against +0.0355 in the submitted version, but the difference is not significant after correction (Table 2), so the paper does not repeat the sentence.
- **LassoBench.** The stored LassoBench runs mixed two versions of the objective and could not be reproduced, so LassoBench is rerun in one environment and the submitted row of Table 4 (Table 3 of the submitted version) is replaced.
- **Geometry.** ZINC-250k is a reference set, not the VAE's training set. The rebuttal called the sample mean norm 7.9846 a measured training-set value; it was measured on ZINC-250k. Sentences that called the shell the decoder's training geometry are removed. The rebuttal also reported the shell radius of the NAS-Bench-101 graph VAE. The paper does not print it, because that embedding could not be retrained reproducibly and two trainings gave different radii.
- **Convex combinations.** The rebuttal explained the gap of the convex-combination samplers by averaging, which moves candidates inside the shell. That explanation does not fit the sampler with k = 1, which draws encoded molecules themselves (Appendix I).
- **Abstract and names.** As promised to the reviewers, the abstract and conclusion state that this is a controlled study of a Gaussian-prior VAE benchmark, not a recommendation to use VAEs for molecular optimization. "Vanilla BO on the sphere" of the submitted version is "the GP on the shell" in the camera-ready version.
- **Random search against the rows that propose off the shell.** The submitted version said that random search on the shell exceeds every classical box-supported comparator and that the direction is consistent across the comparisons, without a test. The camera-ready adds the task-level test at the authors' request, after the results had been read (RUN_RULES row 2026-11-03; `docs/baselines/post_hoc_random_search.md`): random search is higher on most tasks against each of the seven rows, but no difference is significant before or after correction.
- **Table 4 (the cross-domain table; Table 3 of the submitted version).** At the authors decision of 2026-11-03, Table 4 and its full version print a column with the best other method of each benchmark and its mean: the comparators printed in Table 1, the other NAS-Bench-101 methods (random search, TuRBO, a GP and full-dimensional CMA-ES) and the LassoBench rows with every seed of every task. The GP on the shell on GuacaMol and random search on NAS-Bench-101 are level with GeoWalk and GeoCMA; the LassoBench CMA-ES is ahead of GeoCMA. The submitted Table 3 had no such column.
- **Tables 2, 3 and 19 (2026-11-05, authors' decision after their supervisors' review).** The submitted Table 2 held three blocks: the box and shell versions of three GP recipes, the 2x2 cell means of the factorial, and its five contrasts. The camera-ready prints the recipe rows as Table 2, the four cell means as Table 3, and the five contrasts as Table 19 in a new Appendix K (Appendix J since 2026-11-06, see below). The generator still writes the three blocks as one `tab_boxsphere.tex`; the split, the new files `tab_factorial.tex` and `tab_factorial_contrasts.tex` and the shorter captions of Tables 2, 3 and 4 are hand edits recorded in `docs/camera_ready/PAPER_TABLE_EDITS.patch`, and the details that left the captions are in Sections 4.3 and 4.4 of the paper. The appendix tables of Appendices A to I are therefore numbered 5 to 18. No value, test or family changes.
- **Table 1 and Figure 1 (2026-11-06, authors' decision).** The GP on the shell, the GP of the paper's own on-sphere scaffold with candidate-set argmax, is not a baseline, so it is no longer a row of Table 1 (RUN_RULES row 2026-11-06). Table 2 prints it beside its box version; the per-task table of Appendix C, the trajectory table and the convergence figure of Appendix D, the budget table of Appendix F and the published-recipe table of Appendix H keep it as the comparator of GeoWalk, and Result 1 prints the task-level test in the prose. The best other method of Table 4 on GuacaMol is still chosen among the comparators of Table 1 and the GP on the shell, so its entry is unchanged; no generated number changes. The convergence figure (Figure 1) names its curves as Section 4.2 and Tables 1 and 2 do instead of by their implementation, its panel titles no longer carry the per-task count (it is in the figure title), and the caption of Table 2 no longer names a bold rule, because no printed value of that table is bold.
- **Table 3, Table 19 and the Mechanism paragraph (2026-11-06 16:30, authors' decision after a co-author's review).** The shell x continuous cell of the factorial is now the L-BFGS-B arm of control C4 (RUN_RULES row 2026-10-14; `hvarfner_scaled_gp_sphere_lbfgs`, read through the root `CRC4`) in place of projected Adam (`hvarfner_scaled_gp_sphere_continuous`), so that both supports use one continuous maximizer (RUN_RULES row 2026-11-06 16:30). The change was made after the results had been read; every sign and every corrected significance of the five contrasts is unchanged and the effects are smaller (cell 0.3999 instead of 0.3883; shell minus box under continuous maximization +0.0010 on 6 of 11 tasks, p = 0.90, instead of -0.0106 on 5 of 11, p = 0.32; candidate-set argmax minus continuous maximization on the shell +0.0229 instead of +0.0345; interaction +0.0294 on 9 of 11 tasks instead of +0.0409 on 10 of 11). `docs/baselines/c4_reading.md` records both readings. The Mechanism paragraph of Section 4.3 and the paragraph of Appendix J (then lettered K) on the instrumented runs of projected Adam left the paper, because they described the arm that is no longer printed; Section 3.1 describes L-BFGS-B on both supports, and the Limitations and the message of the abstract, introduction and conclusion say "continuous maximization by L-BFGS-B". The solver class, the registration, the manifest entry and the tests of the C4 arm (commit `2a899ad` of the controls clone) are in this repository, so every printed cell can be rerun; `scripts/reproduce_tables.sh` passes the new root. The overview of Appendix H names the maximizers of the DSP GP as candidate-set argmax or L-BFGS-B, and the card of the projected-Adam arm stays a repository record. The availability statement links the project page of the paper.
- **Appendix tables named as Table 1 (2026-11-06, evening; authors' decision).** Tables 7 to 15 and 18 and the notes to Table 1 name the methods as Table 1 and Figure 1 do (TuRBO, BAxUS, Linear BO, the two Linear BO ports, the TuRBO-1 re-implementation, GaBO, the Riemannian Matern GP, REMBO and HeSBO, CMA-ES, the published recipes) instead of by their implementation; the full tables of the repository (`docs/baselines/`) keep the implementation labels, and the notes to Table 1 map the names to them. Appendices D, G and J follow. Wording only, recorded in `docs/camera_ready/PAPER_TABLE_EDITS.patch`.
- **The TuRBO-1 re-implementation leaves the printed appendix; the contrasts are Appendix J (2026-11-06, late evening; authors' decision).** The re-implementation of TuRBO-1 on the fixed box (`turbo_box_faithful`, the TuRBO row of the submitted Table 1) is no longer a row of the trajectory table, a curve of the convergence figure or a row of the overview of Appendix H, because Table 1 prints the published TuRBO run (RUN_RULES row 2026-11-06 21:30); the full Table 1, its card and the trajectory figure of the repository keep it, and every printed value is unchanged. The convergence figure and the appendix tables call the box version of the GP on the shell "GP in the box". The five contrasts of the factorial are Appendix J and the compute appendix is K, so that Table 19 shares a page with text; the appendix letters below follow this order.
- **Hand-edited tables.** Most generated tables of the paper were edited by hand after the final run, only in wording, never in a number: Table 1 and its notes (the mark and the sentence on the published recipes removed on 2026-11-03; the uniform-draw fallbacks of GaBO added), the columns that stated which published recipes reproduced a published result (Tables 14 and 15 and the LassoBench table), the plain-language labels of the five contrasts (now Table 19) and, until 2026-11-05, the sentence on the box run of the MSR recipe with qLogEI in the caption of Table 2 (now in Section 4.3), the mark of the two LassoBench-only arms in Table 13, the headings of the published methods in the LassoBench table and of the published recipes in Table 14, and the caption of Table 4, which defines n in place of the seed numbers and says that bold compares the two means (all 2026-11-03), the unused abbreviations and the note on the tutorial rows in Table 14, and, on the authors rule of 2026-11-03 that a caption says only what the reader needs to read the table, the captions of Tables 1 to 13 and 15 lose their notes on the computing environment, the time limit, how a baseline was rebuilt, repository file paths and run history; on 2026-11-05 the split of Table 2 into Tables 2, 3 and 19 and the shorter captions of Tables 2 to 4 (above); on 2026-11-06, late evening, after a cold reading of the appendix, the row names of Table 9 ("GP in the box", "Random Search (shell)"), the caption pointer and the lift row of Table 5 (the no-op "renormalize" left), the caption of Table 14 ("Matérn-5/2 GP") and the second row of Table 19 ("with L-BFGS-B"); the caption of Table 8, whose two `\ref` commands a scripted edit had broken, was repaired. The edits are in `docs/camera_ready/PAPER_TABLE_EDITS.patch`; `scripts/reproduce_tables.sh` regenerates these tables without them.
